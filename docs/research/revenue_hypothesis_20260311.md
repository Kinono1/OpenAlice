# Revenue Hypothesis

Date: `2026-03-11`

## Core Claim

OpenAlice does not need to become autonomous-live-ready first in order to create economic value.

The nearer-term monetization path is:

- reduce your decision latency
- reduce low-quality discretionary trades
- concentrate your attention on only the highest-quality setups

## What OpenAlice Can Do Now

Right now OpenAlice can already provide:

- structured expert decision synthesis
- cross-check between strategy, ML, and news
- demo-runtime health monitoring
- telegram delivery and web interaction

That is enough to support a `human-in-the-loop` trading workflow.

## What OpenAlice Cannot Honestly Claim Yet

- autonomous live trading readiness
- `G3`-validated statistical edge
- release-gate promotion

## Temporary Revenue Mode

The temporary revenue mode is:

- OpenAlice surfaces only higher-quality candidate situations
- you manually decide whether to deploy capital
- you trade less often, not more often
- you use it to avoid bad trades before trying to find perfect trades

Runtime note:

- trade permission has been confirmed
- non-empty fill recovery has been confirmed
- this improves execution confidence, but does not change the current research failure

## Success Criterion

In the near term, this system is successful if it helps you:

- skip more bad setups
- focus on fewer higher-conviction setups
- avoid impulsive or news-risk-dominated trades
- keep the operational stack stable while research continues

## Failure Criterion

This mode fails if:

- it encourages more low-quality trades
- it becomes a justification for stealth auto-live behavior
- it consumes more time than it saves
- runtime instability keeps interrupting the operator loop

## Strategic Position

This is a bridge mode.

The final target remains:

- pass `G3`
- qualify runtime recovery fully
- then revisit automation

But that is a later milestone, not the current revenue path.
